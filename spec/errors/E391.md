## Description

The replacing text of a `[: text]` is, or contains, `xxx`, `yyy` or
`www`. A replacement exists to give MOR an analysable form; untranscribed
material is by definition not analysable.

## Expected Behavior

`replacement.rs` reports E391 at the replacement when any word of the
replacing text is an untranscribed marker. The markers outside a
replacement are not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

The manual's Replacement scope `[: text]`: "the item being replaced can
only be a single word" that MOR should analyse under its standard form
(https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Replacement_Scope, https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Replacement_Real). `xxx` marks unintelligible speech and `www` untranscribed material
(https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#UnintelligibleSpeech_Code, https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#UntranscribedMaterial_Code).
