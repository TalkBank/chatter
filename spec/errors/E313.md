## Description

An opening parenthesis in word content has no closing partner. Parentheses
in CHAT mark the omitted part of a noncompleted word and untimed pauses, and
both forms are balanced.

## Expected Behavior

The unbalanced parenthesis breaks the word's parse and recovery reports E316
over the unparsable region. E313 exists as a more specific verdict, but no
raw main-tier input reaches its classifier today, so E316 is the observable
diagnostic. A balanced noncompletion `(be)cause` parses as one word.

**Trigger**: See example above

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual's "Noncompletion of a Word" code puts the omitted material in
parentheses inside the word:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Noncompletion_Code>; pauses
are `(.)`, `(..)`, `(...)` or a timed `(2.5)`
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Pause_Marker>). CLAN CHECK
reports an unmatched `(` as its error 28.

## Notes

- Reaching E313 for main-tier content would mean giving the word-content
  recovery classifier a parenthesis balance test, which is a parser change
  (WP4) rather than a spec one; until then this subsumption is documented as
  the current state, not as intended behavior.
