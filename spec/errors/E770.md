## Description

A main-tier timing bullet needs preceding utterance material. Later speech
cannot provide a backward scope for a bullet already encountered. The explicit
nonverbal zero, events, actions and pauses supply material; linkers, precodes,
and structural markers do not. Traverse nested groups in source order.

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual's Audio and Video Time Marks section scopes timing markers to
preceding material: <https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Media_Linking>.
CHECK 73's leading-bullet case corroborates the missing backward scope. This
rule does not copy CHECK's separate legacy multi-bullet option policy and does
not decide empty scopes between later bullets.

## Expected Behavior

Report E770 at every timing bullet encountered before the first material item.
Keep all bullets and their source spans. Suppress this absence claim when the
main tier is parse-recovered, because preceding material may have been lost.

## Observations

CHECK (21-Sep-2026) reports 73 for examples 1, 5, 7 and 8 (twice for 8),
but not for the explicit-zero, event, pause or preceding-group-material
controls. Example 9 reports unmatched-bracket 23 and 73; Chatter reports parse
E375 and suppresses E770 because recovery makes preceding material uncertain.
CHECK additionally reports 157 for all generated fixture basenames because they
differ from the authored media stem. Those filename diagnostics are not the
bullet-rule observation.

All cleanly parsed examples, including the invalid leading-bullet retrace (5),
roundtrip byte-exactly. The group container owns inter-item spaces, so a first
bullet does not introduce an extra space after the opening angle bracket.
The parse-recovered example 9 is not a byte-exact roundtrip claim.
