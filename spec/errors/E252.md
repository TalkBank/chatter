## Description

The syllable-pause marker (`^`) occurs without spoken material on both sides
inside the same word. A leading or trailing caret cannot mark a pause *between*
syllables.

## Expected Behavior

Validation reports E252 for a syllable-pause marker at either edge of a word.
The manual's `rhi^noceros` pattern has spoken material on both sides and does
not trigger E252.

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual, section 9.9, “Prosody Within Words,” defines `^` as a pause
between syllables and illustrates it with `rhi^noceros`:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#X67454>.

## Notes

- Detection is model-level in `validation/word/structure.rs` and examines the
  typed content on both sides of the marker.
- E252 is a TalkBank validator enhancement beyond the audited CLAN CHECK
  diagnostic map; it gives the manual's placement rule a specific location.
