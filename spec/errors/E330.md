**Last updated:** 2026-09-08 22:25 EDT

## Description

The parser's typed walk over the concrete syntax tree met something it could
not decode or dispatch: a node whose text is not valid UTF-8, or a position
where the walk needs a content node and the tree does not have one.

Most of the producers are internal invariants that a correct grammar cannot
violate, and no CHAT text reaches them. Three are different, and are the same
case seen from three dispatchers: when tree-sitter recovers from a malformed
free-text dependent tier by putting an ERROR node where the body belongs, the
reader for that tier reports the ERROR node itself as E316 and then E330 for
the content node it does not have (until 2026-09-08 the E316 came from a walk
the utterance parser ran over every tier before attaching it; the reader owns
both halves now). Which of the three answers depends only on the tier:
`read_tier_body_text` for the text-payload tiers ("Tier is missing content
node", the example above), the shared text-tier body parser for the
bullet-payload tiers such as `%com` ("Missing content in %com tier"), and the
user-defined dispatch for `%x` tiers ("Missing content in user-defined tier
%xfoo").

## Expected Behavior

A file whose dependent-tier body cannot start any alternative of
`text_with_bullets` is rejected, and the pair of codes says both halves of
what happened: E316 names the text nothing could parse, E330 names the typed
walk arriving at a body slot with no content in it.

The remaining producers are documented in their own modules as internal
invariants. `typed_cst::decode_present_child` needs a child whose bytes are
not valid UTF-8, and cannot be handed one because the source of a parse is a
`&str`. The structural arms of the `%pho`, `%mor`, `%sin`, `%wor` and
header-metadata walks stand over rigid sequences the grammar cannot produce
otherwise. The same malformed bullet delimiter, probed through `%mor`, `%gra`,
`%pho`, `%wor`, `@Comment`, `@Situation` and `@Media` on 2026-09-07, reached
none of them: each is intercepted earlier, by the rule's own diagnostic or by
whole-line recovery. Where one of those arms does fire it is a chatter defect
to fix rather than a transcript to correct.

**Trigger**: See the third example above

## CHAT Rule

None of its own: E330 names a state of the parse rather than a CHAT
construct. The constructs the reachable example is about are the time bullet
and the dependent tier
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Dependent_Tiers>); the retrace
group in the first two examples is governed by the "Retracing" scoped symbols
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Retracing_Scope>), whose
unclosed form is E316's business.

## Notes

- Kept as a registered code because a diagnostic is better than a panic when
  an invariant breaks.
- This file claimed until 2026-09-07 that a spec could not demonstrate E330
  from a file, and that every producer was an internal invariant. Both were
  wrong: the third example is the correction, and the reason the claim survived
  is recorded in that example's own notes.
- The reachable case is a real defect in what it reports, not in whether it
  reports: "Tier is missing content node" describes the CST rather than the
  file, where the file's problem is a bullet delimiter that opens nothing. A
  named rule for that construct would demote this example to
  `subsumed_by`, which is the shape of the improvement, not a reason to leave
  the code undemonstrated.
