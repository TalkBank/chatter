## Description

A main tier has a speaker and terminator but no meaningful utterance content
between them. The terminator closes an utterance; it is not itself spoken
content.

## Expected Behavior

The parser builds the empty tier shape and validation reports E306. A tier with
at least one lexical or other meaningful content item does not trigger E306.
Parse-tainted tiers are exempt because recovery can empty the typed content
list even when source content is visibly present.

**Trigger**: See example above

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual describes the main line as the transcription of what the
speaker said, beginning after the speaker ID, colon, and tab:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Main_Line>. A bare terminator does
not provide that required utterance content.

## Notes

- E306 is also used when the typed content list contains only separators.
- The example may produce a more specific empty-word recovery diagnostic as
  well; the E306 claim is that the utterance-level absence is still reported.
