## Description

A main tier has a speaker and terminator but no meaningful utterance content
between them. The terminator closes an utterance; it is not itself spoken
content.

## Expected Behavior

Validation reports E306 for a non-tainted empty tier or authored separator-only
content. A tier with at least one lexical or other meaningful content item does
not trigger E306.
Parse-tainted tiers are exempt because recovery can empty the typed content
list even when source content is visibly present.

A separator inserted as `MISSING` by parser recovery is not authored content.
It must not become an ordinary model separator or emit punctuation when the
recovered model is serialized. Actual separator-only content remains present
and is still subject to E306.

**Trigger**: See example above

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual describes the main line as the transcription of what the
speaker said, beginning after the speaker ID, colon, and tab:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Main_Line>. A bare terminator does
not provide that required utterance content.

## Notes

- E306 is also used when the typed content list contains only separators.
- The terminator-only example reports E342 for parser recovery. Its E306 claim
  is explicitly subsumed rather than supported by a fabricated separator.
