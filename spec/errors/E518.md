## Description

A `@Date` value that is not in the manual's `DD-MMM-YYYY` form: a
one-digit day, slashes, a month that is not a three-letter English
abbreviation in capitals, a day outside 01–31, a spelled-out month, or a
lowercase month. Day and year fields require ASCII digits: letters and
integer signs are not permitted even when their byte counts match.

## Expected Behavior

`check_date_format` reports E518 at the `@Date` for every value that does
not parse as a two-digit day, a three-letter uppercase month and a
four-digit year, or that names a day outside 01–31. Numeric admission is not
general integer parsing: `+1` and `+024` are invalid components. These format
checks are not a claim of full calendar or leap-year validation. The manual
form is not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

"The entry for this header is given in the form day-month-year ...
`@Date: 01-JUL-1965` ... include the full value for the year ... the days
of the month should always have two digits" (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Date_Header).

## Notes

- `@Birth of XXX` uses the same format and the same check.
- The six invalid examples used to place `*CHI:` after `@End`, which added
  E501 and E502 to every observation; they now differ from the legal example
  in the date alone.
