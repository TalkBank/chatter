## Description

An `@Eg:` label does not match the label of the `@Bg:` scope it would
close.

## Expected Behavior

`check_gem_balance` reports E528 at the `@Eg` when a scope is open but not
under this label. Because the `@Eg` then closes nothing, E527 is reported
on it as well, and the open `@Bg` is reported as E526 at the end of the
file. Exactly matching labels are not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual's `@Bg` and `@Eg` headers
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Bg_Header>,
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Eg_Header>): each `@Bg` must
have a matching `@Eg`, and when the colon form is used the text after `@Eg:`
must exactly match the corresponding `@Bg:`. One pair may be nested inside
another, but "double embedding is not allowed". The lazy form `@G`
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#G_Header>) marks gems that
neither nest nor overlap, each running to the next `@G`.

## Notes

- Matching is exact text after the tab: `Story` and `story` are different
  gems.
