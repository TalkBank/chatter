## Description

Shortening parentheses are grammar-owned structure. The two examples retain
the distinct ERROR-node (E316) and missing-token (E342) rejection paths.
Recovered structure is not proof of valid CHAT.

## Expected Behavior

Reject malformed shortening at the parser boundary. Do not reconstruct a
parenthesis stack from Word spelling to add E231: that spelling is derived
from typed structure, not an independent copy of the original source.
Imported orthographic text containing structural parentheses is rejected as
E243; importers must use a typed shortening element instead.

## Notes

E231 remains a historical diagnostic identifier. These active specimens pin
the parser diagnostics that subsume it, without removing recovery evidence or
turning malformed input into a legal control.
