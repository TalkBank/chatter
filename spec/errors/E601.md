## Description

The separator-only dependent-tier fixture is rejected by E316: no typed tier
structure survives at this recovery slot. No tier is reconstructed by scanning
a percent prefix or pipe characters. Valid controls remain legal. Parsed tiers
retain their own typed diagnostics.
