## Description

A `%gra` item's index or head number does not correspond to any item of
the `%mor` tier it annotates (here a head of 5 over a two-word
utterance).

## Expected Behavior

`alignment/gra/align.rs` reports E712 at the `%gra` when an index or head
exceeds the number of `%mor` items (0 is the ROOT head and is always in
range). Indices that all fall within 1 to the item count are not
reported.

**Trigger**: See example above

## CHAT Rule

`%gra` "indicates the dependency structure by giving, for each word, its
index, the index of its head and the relation"
(https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#GrammaticalRelations_Tier); an index names a `%mor` item.
