## Description

The replacing text of a `[: text]` contains an omitted-word marker
(`0word`). A replacement supplies what was said in standard form; an
omission marks what was not said, and cannot be a standard form of
something that was.

## Expected Behavior

The model-owned replacement rule reports E390 when a replacement word has
the omission category. Lowering also applies this rule to a written `0`
prefix whose body is missing, without constructing a model word for it.
An omission outside a replacement is not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

The manual's Replacement scope `[: text]`: "the item being replaced can
only be a single word" that MOR should analyse under its standard form
(https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Replacement_Scope, https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Replacement_Real). Omitted words are marked with a leading `0` (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#OmittedWord_Code).
