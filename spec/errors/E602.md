## Description

A line begins with `%` but is not a dependent tier header: the colon is
missing, or the colon is not followed by a tab.

## Expected Behavior

File-level recovery recognizes the `%`-shaped line and reports E602 over
it (`error_analysis/file.rs`, `chat_file/helpers.rs`). A line of the form
`%xxx:` + tab + content is not reported by this rule, whatever its content
(that is E601's business).

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

"Each dependent tier begins with the percent symbol, a three-letter code,
a colon and a tab" (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Dependent_Tiers).

## Notes

- The main-tier counterpart (`*CHI: hello .` with a space) is E316, and the
  header counterpart (`@Situation: at home`) is E303; the three families
  have three codes for one defect, which a future consolidation may merge.
