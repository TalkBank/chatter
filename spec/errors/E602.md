## Description

Dependent-tier headers require their grammar-defined delimiter. The malformed
examples are rejected by E316 at unstructured file-level recovery slots.
The parser does not reconstruct a tier by scanning a percent prefix, colon,
or tab. The valid control remains legal. E602 is not required for these shapes.
