## Description

An empty word content list has no spoken or annotated word content. E253 also
reports a main tier consisting only of authored separators, which supplies no
word content. A normal nonempty word does not trigger this rule.

## Expected Behavior

The malformed blank main tier is rejected with parser recovery E342, without
inventing a separator or word. The authored comma-only example reports E253
and E306 through model validation. Empty word collections imported through the
model boundary remain subject to E253 independently of parser recovery.

**Trigger**: See example above

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual defines the main line primarily as a series of words and a
word as a nonempty series of permitted characters and word-internal markers:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Words>. E253 is chatter's
structural invariant for a word with none of those items, and also identifies
separator-only utterances with no word content.

## Notes

- Recovery diagnostics do not authorize fabrication of content to preserve a
  secondary validation code. The blank example remains as an explicit
  subsumption control beside the authored separator and lexical controls.
- Successful validation, rather than an unchecked constructor or JSON import,
  is the transition that excludes invalid model states from valid CHAT.
