## Description

The `%mor` tier has fewer items than the main tier has alignable words
(ordinary words, tag markers and the terminator), so the last words have
no analysis.

## Expected Behavior

`alignment/mor.rs` counts the main tier's alignable content and the
`%mor` items and reports E705 at the tier when there are too few. A tier
with one item per alignable word is not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

"The %mor tier ... contains one item for each word on the main tier"
(https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Morphological_Tier); which main-tier material counts is the
alignment rule (retraced material and scoped annotations are excluded).

## Notes

- Its twin is E706 (too many); together they are the count-based
  alignment for `%mor`. Item-by-item alignment defects are other codes.
