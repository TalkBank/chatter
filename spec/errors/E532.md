## Description

A participant role, in `@Participants` or in the role field of `@ID`, is
not one of the roles the manual defines.

## Expected Behavior

The role is checked against the closed role list
(`validation/header/participant.rs::is_allowed_participant_role`) both
where `@Participants` is parsed and where the `@ID` role field is checked,
and E532 is reported at each place it fails. Suggestions use recognizable
substrings to offer likely canonical roles; they are not an edit-distance
search or an automatic normalization. Exact case-sensitive role admission
remains separate from that advice. A manual role is not reported.

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

The manual's role list under `@Participants` (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Participants_Header),
and the `@ID` role field, which takes the same values (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#ID_Header).

## Notes

- The role vocabulary has one owner in chatter; adding a role means
  changing that list, not this spec.
- CHECK21-Sep-2026 observed September23 at19:17 EDT accepts the canonical
  six-role control and emits15 for both the colloquial/misspelled and lowercase
  mutations. It additionally emits142 for several rejected ID roles; Chatter
  does not invent a role mismatch when both authored spellings are identical.
