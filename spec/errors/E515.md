## Description

An `@ID` line's eighth field, the role, is empty.

## Expected Behavior

`check_id_header` reports E515 at the `@ID`. An `@ID` with a role is not
reported by this rule (a role that is not one of the manual's is E532).

**Trigger**: See examples above

## CHAT Rule

The `@ID` role field, "as in the @Participants line" (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#ID_Header).
