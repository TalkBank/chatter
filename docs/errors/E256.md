# E256: Illegal curly single quote

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: word

**Status**: ✅ Active

## Description

A curly single quotation mark (U+2018 or U+2019) is used as a word character.
CHAT requires the ASCII apostrophe; the curly form (typically a typographic
apostrophe introduced by autocorrect or ASR) is not a legal word character.
Mirrors CLAN CHECK errors 138 (U+2019) and 139 (U+2018).

## CHAT Rule

CHAT word text uses ASCII punctuation; curly typographic single quotes are not
legal word characters and should be the ASCII apostrophe. CLAN CHECK 138/139.
CHAT manual: https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html

CHECK (21-Sep-2026) accepts the shortening control and reports 139 for its
left-quote mutation and 138 for its right-quote mutation. Source inspection
matches the observations: `check.cpp::isBadQuotes` (2906–2913) recognizes the
two UTF-8 byte sequences, and the main-tier word scan in `check_CheckWords`
(3197–3199) emits the returned code. The sensible shared policy is character
validity, including inside parenthesized word material; Chatter expresses the
same fault as E256 without adopting CHECK's byte-scanning implementation.
