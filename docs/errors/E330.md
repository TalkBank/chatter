# E330

> Several rules are reported under this code. Each section below is a
> separate specification with its own status.

## Internal CST traversal failure

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: utterance, tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

The parser's typed walk over the concrete syntax tree met something it could
not decode or dispatch: a node whose text is not valid UTF-8, or a position
where the walk needs a content node and the tree does not have one.

Most of the producers are internal invariants that a correct grammar cannot
violate, and no CHAT text reaches them. Three are different, and are the same
case seen from three dispatchers: when tree-sitter recovers from a malformed
free-text dependent tier by putting an ERROR node where the body belongs, the
reader for that tier reports the ERROR node itself as E316 and then E330 for
the content node it does not have (until 2026-09-08 the E316 came from a walk
the utterance parser ran over every tier before attaching it; the reader owns
both halves now). Which of the three answers depends only on the tier:
`read_tier_body_text` for the text-payload tiers ("Tier is missing content
node", the example above), the shared text-tier body parser for the
bullet-payload tiers such as `%com` ("Missing content in %com tier"), and the
user-defined dispatch for `%x` tiers ("Missing content in user-defined tier
%xfoo").

Examples 4–6 keep the same three tier labels and text, inserting only the
bare U+0015 opener into `%com` or `%xnote`. They witness the two other body
dispatchers described above; the unchanged sibling tiers remain available.
Both dispatchers omit the unrecoverable tier while retaining the E316/E330
refusal evidence. A failed comment body must not manufacture an empty `%com`
tier or an E756 validation cascade; a genuinely absent optional body remains
a separate modeled state that validation judges with E756. Neither recovered
model is proof of valid CHAT.

### CHAT Rule

None of its own: E330 names a state of the parse rather than a CHAT
construct. The constructs the reachable example is about are the time bullet
and the dependent tier
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Dependent_Tiers>); the retrace
group in the first two examples is governed by the "Retracing" scoped symbols
(<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#Retracing_Scope>), whose
unclosed form is E316's business.

## Dependent-tier composition and traversal recovery

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

Dependent-tier recovery preserves source-owned attachment and unrelated content.
Cross-tier parse health is not a tier's own retention receipt or a CHAT-validity
claim. Structured MOR rejection, GRA structural placeholders, partial GRA
lowering, free-text body recovery and validation-only empty content have distinct
contracts. The complete control and eleven body-only variants keep those contracts
composed with intact dependent tiers and earlier/later utterances.

### CHAT Rule

MOR items require a POS, pipe and stem; GRA relations require admitted positive
indices and numeric heads. A bare media-bullet opener is not free text. Empty
optional text content remains represented for E756 validation. Healthy native
carriers must remain attached to their own main tier; recovery does not license
invented identities or certainty.
