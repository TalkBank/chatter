# E702

> Several rules are reported under this code. Each section below is a
> separate specification with its own status.

## Unparsable content in a %mor item

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

Unparsable content within a `%mor` item. Missing separators, missing stems,
extra pipes and malformed compound structure are distinct examples; the
diagnostic must not claim that every such item lacks a pipe.

The paired `catfish` examples keep the speech fixed and contrast a complete
single lemma with an extra pipe (CHECK 79) and malformed compound structure
(CHECK 87). A recovery fragment beginning with `|fish` inside an existing
item is not an independently authored empty-POS item: E760 must not replace
the morphology error merely because the CST split the invalid item there.

### Examples

#### Example 1

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
*CHI:	hello world .
%mor:	hello n|world .
@End
```

#### Example 2

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
*CHI:	hello .
%mor:	co| .
@End
```

#### Example 3

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
*CHI:	catfish .
%mor:	n|catfish .
@End
```

#### Example 4

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
*CHI:	catfish .
%mor:	n|cat|fish .
@End
```

#### Example 5

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
*CHI:	catfish .
%mor:	n|cat+n|fish .
@End
```

### CHAT Rule

`%mor` items are `pos|stem` with optional prefix, suffix and translation
fields (https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#MOR_Format).

## Internal recovery within repeated-clitic morphology

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

Unparsable nonempty morphology content retains its MOR context even inside a later post-clitic. This applies the existing E702 recovery rule, not a text-based approximation of a recovered CST. Inspect actual producer states separately: document recovery, native carriers and strict fragments need not retain the same model.

### Examples

#### Example 1

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
@Comment:	Authored MOR/GRA combinations: repeated post-clitics, POS subcategories,
	ordered flat/keyed/multivalue features, Unicode lemmas and a compound lemma.
	The middle turn has four main words and MOR items but seven GRA word chunks,
	including two post-clitics and the terminator. Neighboring turns are sentinels.
*CHI:	start .
%mor:	verb|start .
%gra:	1|0|ROOT 2|1|PUNCT
%com:	before café
*CHI:	we'd've found café icecream .
%mor:	pron:pers|we-Nom-Person=1~aux|have-Tense=Past~aux|have-Mood=Ind-Polarity=Pos verb|find-Past noun|café-Case=Acc noun|ice_cream-PronType=Int,Rel .
%gra:	1|4|NSUBJ 2|4|AUX 3|2|AUX 4|0|ROOT 5|4|OBJ 6|4|OBJ 7|4|PUNCT
%com:	middle naïve
*CHI:	finish .
%mor:	verb|finish .
%gra:	1|0|ROOT 2|1|PUNCT
%com:	after résumé
@End
```
