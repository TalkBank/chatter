# E312: Unclosed bracket

> deprecated

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: utterance

**Status**: deprecated

## Description

The former classifier searched recovery text for an opening square bracket
without a closing bracket. That does not establish a parsed bracket construct
or a missing delimiter. E312 has no live producer and is deprecated.

Malformed fixtures remain rejected by ordinary grammar recovery. Direct
canonical-fixture regression tests retain both invalid examples even though
the code's deprecated status defers its spec-code check. Recovery diagnostics
keep their source locations without asserting an inferred bracket structure.
