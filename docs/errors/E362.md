# E362: Bullet times backwards

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: utterance

**Status**: ✅ Active

## Description

A main-tier media bullet ends at or before its start, or starts earlier than
the preceding main-tier bullet; or a `%wor` word bullet ends before it starts.
A zero-duration word bullet is legal, and word bullets may overlap or start
out of order (leniency policy, Decision 10). Untimed words are legitimate
partial timing.

## CHAT Rule

The CHAT manual's "Time Alignment" scope: the bullet gives the begin and
end of the utterance in milliseconds, and utterances are transcribed in
time order:
<https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.html#TimeAlignment_Scope>. CLAN
CHECK's error 83 covers the ordering half.
