# E764: prefixed form glued to the preceding word

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Severity**: error

**Status**: ✅ Active

## Description

The & prefixes introduce a word of their own: a filler (&-um), a nonword (&~gaga), or a phonological fragment (&+fr). Each is a separate main-tier word and must be separated from what precedes it by a space:

## Examples

### Example 1

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Target_Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Target_Child|||
*CHI:	the dog&-um barked .
@Comment:	ERROR: a missing space silently split this into two words
@End
```
