# E720

> Several rules are reported under this code. Each section below is a
> separate specification with its own status.

## Mor-Gra count mismatch

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

The number of `%mor` chunks does not equal the number of `%gra` relations
for an utterance. `%gra` aligns 1-to-1 with `%mor` chunks (not items, a
`%mor` item with post-clitics produces multiple chunks).

### Examples

#### Example 1

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Target_Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|2;06.|male|||Target_Child|||
@Comment:	Note: %gra aligns to %mor chunks, not items!
*CHI:	I want cookie .
%mor:	pro|I v|want n|cookie .
%gra:	1|2|NSUBJ 2|0|ROOT 3|2|OBJ 4|4|DEP 5|2|PUNCT
@Comment:	ERROR: %mor has 4 chunks (3 words + terminator) but %gra has 5 relations
@End
```

#### Example 2

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child, MOT Mother
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|4;00.||||Child|||
@ID:	eng|corpus|MOT|||||Mother|||
*CHI:	it's a cookie .
%mor:	pron|it~aux|be-Pres-S3 det|a noun|cookie .
%gra:	1|4|NSUBJ 2|1|AUX 3|4|DET 4|0|ROOT 5|4|PUNCT
@End
```

### CHAT Rule

See CHAT manual sections on dependent tier formats (%mor, %gra). Each
`%mor` chunk requires a corresponding `%gra` relation; clitics in `%mor`
produce additional chunks that also need relations.

## Repeated-clitic chunk and relation boundaries

> ✅ Active; This check is active in the validator.

**Kind**: Invalidity

**Level**: tier

**Status**: ✅ Active

### Description

GRA aligns to expanded MOR word chunks, including each repeated post-clitic and the terminator. Removing or adding an interior or terminal relation cannot be hidden by the number of MOR items. Additional index/head findings remain independent; the claim does not require diagnostic exclusivity.

### Examples

#### Example 1

```chat
@UTF8
@Begin
@Languages:	eng
@Participants:	CHI Child
@ID:	eng|corpus|CHI|||||Child|||
@Comment:	Authored MOR/GRA combinations: repeated post-clitics, POS subcategories,
	ordered flat/keyed/multivalue features, Unicode lemmas and a compound lemma.
	The middle turn has four main words and MOR items but seven GRA word chunks,
	including two post-clitics and the terminator. Neighboring turns are sentinels.
*CHI:	start .
%mor:	verb|start .
%gra:	1|0|ROOT 2|1|PUNCT
%com:	before café
*CHI:	we'd've found café icecream .
%mor:	pron:pers|we-Nom-Person=1~aux|have-Tense=Past~aux|have-Mood=Ind-Polarity=Pos verb|find-Past noun|café-Case=Acc noun|ice_cream-PronType=Int,Rel .
%gra:	1|4|NSUBJ 2|4|AUX 3|2|AUX 4|0|ROOT 5|4|OBJ 6|4|OBJ 7|4|PUNCT
%com:	middle naïve
*CHI:	finish .
%mor:	verb|finish .
%gra:	1|0|ROOT 2|1|PUNCT
%com:	after résumé
@End
```
